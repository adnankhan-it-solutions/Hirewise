# Professional review checklist

For qualified Qatar counsel, the acquirer and accountant; no legal conclusions are made by this repository.

| Item | Required confirmation |
|---|---|
| Commercial/recruitment activity | Entity incorporation, permitted recruitment/service activity, licenses and disclosures |
| Qatar personal data law | Applicability and obligations under authoritative Arabic law/regulations, controller/processor roles |
| Candidate/recruiter notices | Specific purposes, lawful bases, rights, contact, recipients, retention and versioned records |
| Special-nature information | Whether submitted CVs may contain special-nature data; minimization and any permissions/extra controls |
| Cross-border transfers | Hosting/storage/mail/AI regions, subprocessors, contracts and permitted transfer arrangement |
| AI employment use | Explainability, human review, fairness, restrictions and candidate disclosure/objection process |
| Employment requirements | Lawful job criteria, work authorization checks, prohibited discrimination and advertising |
| Retention/rights | Durations per jurisdiction/workflow, legal holds, erasure/export duties and response deadlines |
| Breach response | Notification triggers, recipients, deadlines and evidence preservation |
| Merchant processing | QCB-regulated/acquirer-compatible onboarding, cards/3DS/settlement/refunds and agreement |
| Tax/invoices | Legal business details, numbering, currency, tax applicability/rates and record retention |
| Contracts | Recruiter agreements, candidate terms, acceptable use, responsibility and liability allocation |

Sources for counsel to verify (checked 2026-09-11): NCSA English translation of Law No.13 of 2016, which defers to the Official Gazette version: https://assurance.ncsa.gov.qa/sites/default/files/library/2020-11/Law%20No.%20%2813%29%20of%202016%20%20on%20Protecting%20Personal%20Data%20Privacy%20-%20English.pdf ; QCB Payment Services Regulation: https://www.qcb.gov.qa/Services/Financial%20Technology/PSR%20Final%20-%20English.pdf . These links are context, not certification of compliance.
