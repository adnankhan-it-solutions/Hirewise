# Recruiter guide

Register as a recruiter, verify email and create a company. Administrator approval is required before vacancies can publish. A company owner can invite teammates by email; invitees need a verified recruiter account with the exact invited email. Other companies' applicants are unavailable even if their UUID is known.

Create vacancy → save draft → add weighted job-related criteria → submit for moderation. Editing content/criteria returns the role to draft and increments policy version. Pause/close/duplicate are available. Sandbox packages demonstrate payment flow but do not buy real services.

Applications appear after candidate email verification. Blind review hides identity in initial screens; original CVs may identify the person, and current blind mode is not a fully redacted-document system. Quarantined or infected documents cannot be downloaded. Clean document downloads are short-lived and tied to your session. Screening is the explicitly labelled local evidence baseline; verify claims manually.

Add internal notes, record a stage with a reason, request a text interview with approved questions/rubric, and offer company interview slots from the dashboard. Review answers and record a human assessment after completion. The report presents stored evidence/history; print to PDF using your browser. Analytics shows current stage counts; company owners can export a limited CSV summary. No claim is made yet for complete time-to-hire or cohort reporting.
